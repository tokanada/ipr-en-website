Will it work on my phone?

  • Android 10 or newer.
  • A 64-bit ARM phone (arm64-v8a / ARMv8). Almost every phone from the last several years is.
  • About 40 MB free for the translation data, plus room for the game (about 200 MB for the app plus game data).
  • An internet connection.

Not supported: iPhone, iPad, and 32-bit Android devices.

Emulators (BlueStacks, MuMu, LDPlayer, etc.) are not supported at the moment. Support is being worked on.

Not sure about your phone? Install a free app like "Droid Hardware Info" or "CPU-Z". It should show arm64-v8a or AArch64.

Good to know

  • In-app purchases don't work in the patched game. Purchases you already made are kept.
  • Google and Apple linking don't work. Use Twitter (X) or a password link.
  • Don't install the Korean HoshimiLocalify module or other patched builds alongside this one.
  • Switching between Lite and Full, or Standard and Side-by-side, may need an uninstall. Link your account first.
  • The Side-by-side app has its own separate login and data.

How to install

  1. Link your game account first

    Before you uninstall anything, open the account linking or data transfer menu in the Japanese game. Link your account with Twitter (X) or a transfer password. Guest accounts that aren't linked lose all progress.

    You can skip this for Side-by-side builds, or if you've never played.

    Google account and Apple account linking do NOT work in the patched game. (Apple linking isn't available on Android anyway.) Use Twitter (X) or a password link instead. If your account is linked only to Google, add a Twitter or password link before you switch.

  2. Uninstall the Play Store version

    Standard builds only. The patched app is signed differently, so it can't install over the original. Skip this step for Side-by-side.

  3. Download your APK

    Pick one from the downloads. Wi-Fi is best, the file is about 200 MB.

  4. Open the downloaded file

    If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager.

  5. If Google Play Protect warns you

    Tap More details, then Install anyway.

  6. Launch the game

    The first launch downloads game data. That's normal. Restore your account with Twitter (X) or your transfer password (not Google), then enjoy the game in English.

Updating

When the game says it needs an update, do not update from the Play Store.

Come back here and download the newest build of the same version you installed (Lite or Full, Standard or Side-by-side). Install it over the existing app. Don't uninstall, and your data stays. New translations arrive the same way.

FAQ

Can I get banned?

It's possible. A modified client breaks the game's Terms of Service. No bans have been reported so far, but we can't promise anything. Use it at your own risk.

Is my account safe? Will I lose progress?

Your progress is safe if you link your account before switching. Use Twitter (X) or a transfer password. Unlinked guest accounts lose everything when you uninstall.

Google and Apple linking don't work in the patched game. If you're only linked to Google, add a Twitter or password link first. Side-by-side builds don't touch your original app, but they have their own separate login.

Can I use an emulator?

Not yet. Emulators like BlueStacks, MuMu and LDPlayer aren't supported at the moment. Support is being worked on.

Why does Android warn me?

The app doesn't come from the Play Store, so Android and Google Play Protect show a warning. Tap More details, then Install anyway. You may also need to allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager.

The game says it needs an update

Don't use the Play Store. Come back to this page and download the newest build of the same version you have, then install it over the old one. Your data stays.

Can I use this on iPhone?

No. The patch only works on Android phones and tablets (Android 10 or newer, 64-bit).
